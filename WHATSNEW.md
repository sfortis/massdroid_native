### Added
- Android TV: Home shows the same Discover feed as the phone, with Smart Mix and Genre Radio.
- Android TV: the mini player stays at the bottom of the screen, one press down away.
- The phone speaker connects securely to Music Assistant 2.10 and later, and pairs by itself.
- Sync speakers: set a delay for each speaker in a group.
- Dynamic playlists keep playing after a song you tap, with a switch in the full player.
- Search: genres that match what you type come first, and a tap plays the genre.

### Improved
- Android Auto: Smart Mix and Genre Radio start without opening the app, and the car tells you when a mix cannot play.
- The phone speaker stays closer in time with the other speakers in a group.
- Output calibration runs in rounds and can be fine-tuned for each output.
- A skipped song is no longer hidden for good; the skip fades over time. "Not for me" still hides it.
- One genre spelled several ways, such as synth pop and synth-pop, is now a single genre.
- Insights merges duplicate artists and ranks every list by plays.
- Settings share one clean layout across every screen.
- Follow Me uses less battery, and rests while car audio is connected.
- Sheets and dialogs stay open when you rotate the phone.

### Fixed
- Android Auto shows your library when it starts the app itself, also over a VPN, and large libraries no longer run out of memory. (#78)
- Android Auto no longer starts music on a speaker at home when the car connects.
- Music on the phone resumes after a call when Follow Me is on.
- The connection to Music Assistant comes back by itself after a call or a network drop.
- The phone speaker recovers when an alarm or a lost audio route takes the sound. (#68, #69)
- Grouped playback in Opus no longer plays early.
- The full player's buttons fit in landscape.
