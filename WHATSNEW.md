### Added
- Search: a Library chip searches only your own library until you turn it off.
- Android Automotive: the car settings have an About section with the app version and the privacy policy.

### Improved
- Search shows matches from your library first while the streaming services are still being searched. (#76)
- Items without artwork show a placeholder instead of an empty square, on the phone and on Android TV.
- The home screen widget uses less battery when the music changes quickly.

### Fixed
- Search is more reliable with slower streaming services such as Spotify: a slow answer gets one more try before No results shows. (#76)
- Scrolling the queue back to the top no longer closes it.
- Android Automotive: the car shows a Sign in button when no account is signed in, and signing out clears the car's lists and the playing track at once.
