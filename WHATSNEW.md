### Added
- Party: an admin can choose which player the party plays on, from the Party screen on the phone and in the car settings, and the Party screen shows which player Auto picks. (#85)
- Android Automotive: a Party page in the car settings shows the guest join code while parked, so passengers can add songs. (#85)
- Android Automotive: tap the artist or album on the playing screen to open it. (#86)

### Improved
- Tablets: Now Playing in landscape shows the cover beside larger controls, and lists, settings and Party keep to a comfortable width. (#89)
- Android Automotive: the car settings are grouped into Server, Guest access, Player and About. (#85)
- Genres are more accurate for artists who share their name with another artist.
- Follow Me recognizes a room even when one of its beacons is silent.

### Fixed
- A watch's play and pause buttons now control the selected player. (#88)
- The car's instrument cluster no longer shows the previous track's cover. (#84)
- The artist page no longer crashes on Music Assistant servers older than 2.9. (#87)
- Music comes back in the car after a voice assistant or another interruption pauses it.
- Follow Me selects the room's player even after Android restarted the app in the background.
